This website sets no tracking cookies

This website does not use advertising, profiling or tracking cookies, so it does not show a cookie banner. Visit statistics are collected with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts page views without cookies and without identifying you.

Fonts are served from this website itself, so no third party receives your IP address when the page loads.

Third-party pages

When you pay, book a call or fill in the intake form, you leave this website for Stripe, Cal.com or Tally. The link to the 402Scope observatory takes you to 402scope.org. Those services may set their own strictly necessary cookies, described in their own policies.

Changes

If cookies that need your consent are ever added, this page will be updated and you will be asked for consent before they are set.
