MiCA Readiness Review

Project Alba, a staking token preparing to market to EU users. Public materials reviewed against MiCA, AML, consumer and data protection rules.

Summary

Not ready to market to EU users yet. Three fixes block the launch. All three are in marketing copy and can be fixed in about a week. The custody question needs a decision before the white paper is notified.

Three priorities

  1. Remove the fixed-yield promise from the staking page and every social post that repeats it (findings H1 and M6).
  2. Add the risk statement and the MiCA disclaimer next to every call to action (H2, H3).
  3. Decide who holds staked tokens. If the team wallet keeps custody for users, use an authorised provider or assess authorisation (M3).

Redlines

Suggested wording for the high findings. Deleted text in red, new text in blue.

H1 · Staking page, heroMiCA Art. 7
Lock your ALBA for 90 days and earn a guaranteed 18% APY receive staking rewards. Rewards vary with network activity and can fall to zero.

A guaranteed return is not fair, clear and not misleading when rewards depend on the network. The same claim appears in six posts (M6).

H2 · Staking page, above "Stake now"MiCA Art. 7
Staking locks your tokens for 90 days. You can lose all the value of the tokens you stake, and you may not be able to sell them during the lock-up.

The risk must be visible where the decision is made, not only in the white paper.

H3 · Website footer and every marketing postMiCA Art. 7
This crypto-asset marketing communication has not been reviewed or approved by any competent authority in any Member State of the European Union. The offeror of the crypto-asset is solely responsible for its content.

MiCA requires this statement in marketing communications for crypto-assets other than ARTs and EMTs.

Scope and limitations

This review covers the public materials listed on the cover, as they stood on the review date, against EU law and Spanish rules where relevant. Jurisdictions outside the EU are flagged, not analysed. It is a regulatory analysis, not legal advice or a legal opinion, and it does not assess the technical security of smart contracts, investment merits or tax treatment.

This is a sample report about a fictional project, written to show the format of a real review.
